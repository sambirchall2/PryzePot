Match Rules

By creating or joining a PryzePot match, you agree to follow all platform rules.

Players must use the Clash Royale account connected to their PryzePot profile.

All matches must be played as official friendly battles between the two matched players.

Disconnects, exploits, cheating, account sharing, or intentional manipulation of match results may result in removal from the platform.

PryzePot reserves the right to review match results and resolve disputes when necessary.

By continuing, you acknowledge that entry fees will be locked once a match is accepted.
